## Problem Statement

The evaluator stops at the edge of the take-home. Today the pipeline is: GitHub → evidence-scored evaluation → verdict → AI interview questions. Then the funnel ends. The next real step in ColoredCow's hiring process — the **take-home assignment** (Step 2 of the engineering pipeline: a 7-day project candidates work with whatever tools they choose, including AI) — is still handled by hand: someone picks a generic brief, emails it, and tracking lives in email threads. The brief is unpersonalized (same problem for every candidate, regardless of what their evaluation shows), ungrounded (nothing ties it to ColoredCow's actual work), and unobserved (no record of what was sent, when, and whether it was submitted).

There is a second, quieter problem: **the take-home is where candidate quality is actually proven** — the evaluation predicts, the take-home confirms. A tool that generates, dispatches, and tracks a personalized take-home extends the differentiator (behavior→evidence) one step further down the funnel, exactly where no competitor goes.

- The process already exists and is contractual: ColoredCow's Step 2 is a 7-day take-home for all roles; design roles get an anonymized brief from a current client engagement; no-DSA, AI-welcome, "you'll be actually working with us" framing. The feature productizes what ColoredCow already does.
- ColoredCow's portfolio is public, named, and reusable (no anonymized logos on the case-study page — "if a partnership isn't ready to be named yet, it doesn't appear here"): Plio (6-year education-NGO platform, 50k+ learners, multi-tenant, WhatsApp nudges), Aam Digital (Digital Public Good, offline-first case management, 50+ NGOs across 4 continents), CII (AWS estate since 2019), Goonj (CiviCRM+WordPress, 500,000+ contacts), Dost Education (IVR delivery to 130K+ caregivers, ~70% cloud-cost reduction, conversational-Hindi AI voice agent), plus Glific, Avni, Kotahi/Ketida, Tehri Telemedicine, PM Gati Shakti, MFM (D2C). Boilerplates (laravel-init, reactnative-init, wordpress-init, nextjs-init), packages (laravel-gsuite, laravel-audit, civirazorpay, SesPress, gchat-mcp-server), the `portal` Laravel app (335 issues), and `ColoredCow/engineering-recipes` (the reusable practices repo, incl. their Claude automated-code-review recipe). This is grounding material the feature can cite without inventing.
- Business goal mapped: the intrapreneurial ask (Prateek's "solves a real problem, involves real users") and the product-minded ask (the assignment+submission flow becomes a differentiator) both route through this feature.

## Status Quo

What happens today, per ColoredCow practice: HR/tech manually composes or reuses a take-home brief; candidate completes it over ~7 days; submission is reviewed (engineering does a code-review pass; the debrief conversation is part of the values-fit story); trial candidates move to a paid work trial. Manual composition means: (1) the brief doesn't reference the candidate's evaluated evidence (their weak dimensions, their languages, their strengths), (2) it doesn't reflect ColoredCow's project repertoire or work methods, (3) nothing is tracked in the tool, (4) no candidate-facing submission surface exists. Cost today: drift in candidate experience, unreviewable consistency, and a gap in the evaluator's promise.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Immediate user:** HR at ColoredCow — sends a generated brief to a shortlisted candidate by clicking one button; reviews submission status on the candidate show page.
- **Candidate:** receives a personalized brief via the existing no-auth flow, submits a repo URL + short reflection (mirrors the AI-native expectation: "show us your approach, your communication, your decisions").
- **Decision-maker:** Prateek Narang (founder gate).
- **Narrowest wedge:** the auto-gate on verdict (user-chosen): candidates whose evaluation ends `hire`/`strong_hire` get an assignment generated for them as part of the same queue tail that currently generates interview questions. No new UI surface beyond the candidate show page and one submission route. Weeks, not months.

## Constraints

- Laravel 13 / PHP 8.3, SQLite, Blade/Tailwind/Alpine, Gemini→OpenAI fallback, `evaluations` database queue. No auth yet (`Auth::id()` null; `submitted_by`/`hr_user_id` null).
- Evaluation runs **after** analysis + evidence-validation + verdict, in `EvaluateCandidateJob`; assignment generation rides that same job tail (alongside `GenerateInterviewQuestionsJob`).
- AI must not hand out verdicts or grades. The take-home is reviewed by humans (engineering code-review pass + debrief). The feature generates, dispatches, and tracks — it does not score submissions (auto-grading is explicitly rejected, see Approach B).
- Personalization grounding must stay truthful: briefs cite candidate evidence (evaluation + `artifact_file_paths`/`commit_samples`/`languages` on `RepositoryAnalysis`) and ColoredCow's real portfolio patterns — no invented facts about ColoredCow's projects.
- Gemini on `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (GitHub Models + `gemini-3.6-flash` are dead/503 — the free tier that works). Prompt must fit ~128k-token context; JSON-only output with markdown-fence stripping (same discipline as `InterviewQuestionService::parseResponse`).

## Premises

1. Auto-gate on verdict (`hire`/`strong_hire`) is the right trigger — it extends the existing "questions fire after successful evaluation" hook and keeps HR from generating assignments for candidates they'll reject anyway. — user-chosen, agreed
2. The assignment brief must be **personalized from the candidate's own evaluation evidence** (weak dims → focus areas; languages, artifact references, complexity estimate) — otherwise it's a static library, which is not a product. — agreed
3. Grounding in ColoredCow's real project repertoire + culture/work methods is what makes briefs feel like real ColoredCow work (matching the "anonymized brief from a current engagement" practice). Requires a new **portfolio/context source** — `ColoredCowContext` today has values + tech stack + practices/system types but **no project data yet**; the WordPress gap is **confirmed real** (Goonj CRM, SesPress, api-connector-wordpress are all CiviCRM/WordPress, yet WordPress is absent from the context) — the research section above is the curated grounding the new source will carry. — agreed
4. Submissions are reviewed by humans. The feature stores submission metadata + a reflection; it does NOT rank or grade. — agreed
5. The feature closes the evaluator's funnel (evaluate → questions → take-home), which is the product-minded differentiator. — agreed

**Superseded/rejected during session:** "generate for all candidates" (floods HR — user picked verdict-gate); "static brief library" (no personalization, no differentiation); "auto-graded submissions" (erodes trust + contradicts ColoredCow's human review culture).

## Landscape Findings

- Take-home generators exist but sit in the crowded "skills assessment" lane (TestGorilla, CodeSignal, Codility, HackerRank) — standardized libraries, not personalized briefs. They measure skill compliance, not work-style fit.
- Nothing observed ties the take-home to (a) a candidate's evidence trail and (b) a company's real project portfolio. ColoredCow's own practice (anonymized real-client brief; AI-welcome; "the brief is the work you'd actually do") is exactly the positioning this feature automates.
- The evaluator's existing differentiator — behavior→values evidence, not self-report — extends cleanly to assignment generation: the brief is built from the same evidence the verdict used. Coherent product story, no new infra.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Personalized Take-Home Assignment Generator (chosen spine)

Two-mode feature, both auto-firing at the same hook (`EvaluateCandidateJob::handle`, beside `GenerateInterviewQuestionsJob`), for verdict `hire`/`strong_hire`:

1. **Assignment Brief Generation** — `AssignmentGenerationService` (mirrors `InterviewQuestionService`): builds a prompt from
   - candidate evidence (evaluation score table → the 2-3 weakest dims as focus areas; `primary_languages`; `artifact_file_paths` + `commit_samples` as "what you'd actually look like working on this"; `code_complexity_estimate`),
   - ColoredCow context (6 ColValues, tech stack **including WordPress**, practices, system types) + **new portfolio layer** (real archetypes from the research section: WhatsApp/NGO comms, interactive-video learning, CiviCRM+WP at national scale, offline-first field case-management, D2C e-commerce, AWS estate, OS publishing),
   - a spec of the brief contract. **The contract is anchored to the Software Engineer page's own language**, track-aware (Experienced/Junior/Intern scale difficulty): objective, context ("a real, small project that reflects the kind of work you'd actually do here"), deliverables, review criteria (ColValues + ColoredCow's actual engineering hygiene — tests, CI, docs, code-review-readiness; AI-welcome but "AI as thinking partner, not shortcut"), 7-day timebox, submission instructions (repo URL + reflection on approach/communication/decisions).
   Parses strict JSON (fence-stripped, same discipline as `InterviewQuestionService::parseResponse`), stores one row in a new `assignments` table. On provider failure: log + leave status `error` (never a fake brief). Single AI call per candidate.
2. **Dispatch + Tracking + Submission** — new `assignments` table (candidate_id, brief JSON columns, status machine `generated → dispatched → submitted → under_review → complete`, due_at = +7d from dispatch, submitted_repo_url, reflection, times). HR dispatches via the candidate show page (a "Send take-home" card with the brief preview, copy/send action). Candidate submits via a token-less **public route** (no auth → use a nonce) that renders the brief and collects repo URL + 200-word reflection — matching the AI-native stance ("how you approached it, how you communicated").
   - **Effort:** M — table + migration, service (reuses `AiProviderClient::callWithFallback`, prompt disciplines, existing context services), job, 2 small blades (show-page card + public submission page), 1 public route.
   - **Risk:** Low–Med — the AI-brief quality edge (must stay truthful about ColoredCow facts; portfolio data must be curated, not hallucinated), and the no-auth submission surface (needs a nonce/token, not truly open).
   - **Pros:** Products ColoredCow's own practice; personalized; closes the funnel; both intrapreneurial and product-minded value; reuses everything.
   - **Cons:** New table + public route; requires curating portfolio- archetype data into the app (small, but a real content task); no automatic grading (by design).

### Approach B: Assignment Generator with AI Auto-Grading (rejected)

Same generation, plus the tool scores submissions against the brief (rubric → AI evaluation, like the candidate evaluation itself).

- **Effort:** L — needs submission-fetching (git clone / repo API), a second evidence/validator layer, and a grader prompt + storage.
- **Risk:** High — auto-grading a take-home is the exact trust erosion the human code-review culture avoids; a scored submission verdict would be argued with, unlike a debrief conversation. Contradicts the "evidence, not verdicts-from-AI" positioning of the approved behavioral doc.
- **Pros:** Fully automated loop; more impressive demo.
- **Cons:** Wrong trust model; likely to be rejected by engineering at ColoredCow; heaviest lift for the least defensible payoff. **Decision: deferred** — a lightweight "submission received" + human review notes column is the phase-1 cap.

### Approach C: Static Take-Home Library (rejected)

A curated set of briefs ("build a Laravel CRUD app", "fix this bug") chosen per candidate.

- **Effort:** S.
- **Pros:** Trivial.
- **Cons:** Not personalized, not grounded, not a product story; candidates still get the generic experience; no differentiator vs. TestGorilla.

### Approach D: Portfolio Context Enrichment as a prereq (ride-along)

Add real project archetype data (name, sector, stack, "what working on this means") + WordPress to `ColoredCowContext` (or a new `ColoredCowPortfolio` service) so the prompt layer can cite real work truthfully. The archetype set is now sourced directly from the research section (Plio-type interactive-video learning, Goonj-type CiviCRM+WP CRM at national scale, MFM-type D2C, Aam Digital-type offline-first DPG, Dost-type field/IVR + analytics, CII-type AWS estate, Glific-type WhatsApp two-way, Kotahi/Ketida-type OS publishing, portal-type internal Laravel tool) — curated from named case studies with real numbers, plus WordPress added to the tech stack.

- **Effort:** S (curated array + tests).
- **Risk:** None — pure content that other prompt builders (evaluator, interview questions) can also reuse. Content risk is curation-only: safe-to-cite set and anonymization stance are decided up front (Open Question 1) so generation never alludes to clients in a way that leaks.
- **Pros:** Unblocks Approach A's grounding premise; fixes the documented WordPress gap.
- **Cons:** Needs the user's sign-off on the curated list (which projects are safe to allude to in a candidate-facing brief).
- **Decision: included as part of Approach A's first PR**, not a separate phase.

## Recommended Approach

**A as the spine, D built inside A's PR, B explicitly deferred, C rejected.** Sequence within the build:

1. Portfolio context source (D) + WordPress into tech stack — content + tests, reusable by all prompt builders.
2. `assignments` table migration + model (status machine, due_at, brief JSON, submission fields, nonce/token column).
3. `AssignmentGenerationService` + prompt builder (evidence grounding + culture/portfolio grounding + brief contract) → `AiProviderClient`, strict JSON parse.
4. `GenerateTakeHomeAssignmentJob` dispatched from `EvaluateCandidateJob::handle` when verdict in `{hire, strong_hire}` (same `evaluations` queue, ~120s timeout, tries=1 like questions).
5. Show-page card (brief preview + status timeline + "Send" action) + public submission route/nonce + candidate show status pill.
6. Tests: generation parsing (fenced/dimension-keyed tolerances like CitationValidator), verdict-gate routing, status machine transitions, nonce submission. External AI calls not mocked in existing tests — keep the "input→parsed shape" unit tests + a deterministic brief-contract validation test.

Rationale: it products a practice ColoredCow already runs, closes the evaluator funnel for both the intrapreneurial and product-minded reads, reuses the proven prompting/AI-plumbing patterns, and defers only the trust-risky part (auto-grading).

## Open Questions

1. Curated portfolio list: which real projects may a candidate-facing brief allude to (by name vs. "anonymized pattern")? ColoredCow's own practice is **anonymized real-client briefs** (product-designer role) — the default is anonymization-by-archetype ("an NGO field-communication platform" rather than "Glific"), with named-reference opt-in being the exception. Default stance recorded: **anonymize; match the arc, cite the numbers as a class**. User to confirm the safe set for named references.
2. Submission surface without auth: is a per-assignment nonce (URL like `/assignments/{nonce}`) acceptable as the only gate, or does the candidate-session feature land first?
3. Does dispatch stay HR-click ("Send" button) or auto-email on generation? (No mailer wired yet — HR-click is the phase-1 assumption.)
4. Should `maybe` verdicts get an optional manual "generate anyway" path? (Phase-1: no; note as a one-line toggle if HR asks.)
5. Does the brief need to feed back into the evaluation (e.g., "assignment outcome" column on the candidate) — or is tracking enough? (Phase-1: tracking only.)

## Success Criteria

**Build-this-week criteria (matches pilot sequencing — do not slip into the pilot window):**
- After a `hire`/`strong_hire` evaluation, a candidate has exactly one `assignments` row with a complete, valid brief JSON (contract keys present: objective, context, deliverables, review_criteria, timebox, ai_use_note, submission; track_fit derived from candidate depth so Experienced/Junior/Intern difficulty scales).
- The brief demonstrably references candidate evidence (≥1 focal dimension from their evaluation) and ≥1 real ColoredCow archetype (from the curated portfolio, anonymized).
- Brief language echoes the Software Engineer page contract ("a real, small project that reflects the work you'd actually do here"; AI-welcome with the thinking-partner-not-shortcut caveat; judged on approach/communication/decisions).
- Review criteria in the brief mirror ColoredCow's real engineering hygiene (tests, CI, docs, code-review-readiness — the qualities their own automated code-review recipe expects), not abstract scoring.
- HR can open the show-page card, read the brief, and mark it dispatched; a candidate can submit repo URL + reflection through the nonce route; status advances `generated → dispatched → submitted → under_review → complete`.
- No auto-grading exists in the shipped surface. Submission review is a human note on the candidate.
- Full phpunit suite green (+ new tests), Pint clean.

**Deferred (out of this sprint):** auto-grading; auto-email; assignment-outcome feed into evaluation; optional generate-for-`maybe`.
